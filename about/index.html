COMPANY STORY

Real Problems. Real Software.

MAYYSH TECH is a software product company focused on building practical technology for real-world problems.

Why We Build

Rejecting Bloat. Engineering for Ground Reality.

Modern software has become overly complicated. Simple administrative tasks now require multi-tenant cloud setups, expensive ongoing subscriptions, and fragile internet links. When the internet drops, schools cannot collect fees, clinics cannot check in patients, and businesses stall.

MAYYSH TECH was founded to challenge this assumption. We build software products that put control, speed, and privacy back into the hands of the organization. Software that runs on your computer, owns its data, and continues operating under real-world constraints.

OUR BASE

Rooted in Patan. Built for the World.

Our engineering operations are based in Patan, Gujarat. Being based in a growing regional hub keeps us grounded in the actual daily challenges faced by schools, clinics, and businesses across India. We design products for this real world — with the technical rigor to scale globally.
