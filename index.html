WHY OFFLINE

Internet band, tab bhi school chalu.

Zyada tar school software cloud par chalta hai — internet gaya to kaam ruk jaata hai. Era Campus aapke apne computer par chalta hai.

Cloud-Based School Software

  • Har waqt internet chahiye
  • Monthly ya yearly hosting bill
  • Data company ke server par
  • Server down = kaam band

Era Campus (Offline)

  • Bina internet ke poora chalta hai
  • Ek baar setup — koi recurring bill nahi
  • Data sirf aapke apne computer par
  • Aapka data, aapke control mein, hamesha
