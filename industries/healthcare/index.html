CLINICAL ADMINISTRATIVE INTEGRITY

Practical Architecture for Clinics, Labs & Hospitals

In clinical administration, confusion at the desk creates stress for both patients and medical staff. Mayysh Care provides clean operational clarity.

01 • Outpatient Registration

Instant Patient Intake & Tokens

Register incoming patients in seconds, assign unique medical record numbers, and generate thermal token slips for consultation queues.

02 • Consultation Scheduling

Doctor Queues & Room Allocations

Prevent waiting room congestion with active doctor schedule boards and clear patient call sequences across departments.

03 • Transparent Billing

Consultation, Diagnostics & Invoices

Generate clear, itemized invoices covering outpatient fees, diagnostic tests, and procedural charges without accounting errors.

04 • Dispensary Stock Controls

Pharmacy Inventory & Expiry Tracking

Track medicine stock, automatically flag upcoming batch expirations, and ensure smooth dispensary billing.
